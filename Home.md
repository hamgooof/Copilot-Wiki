## How to use this wiki

- Follow the learning path if terms such as context window, tool or subagent are unfamiliar.
- Open the technology section when deciding how to encode a particular workflow or rule.
- Check the linked official sources before relying on behaviour that may have changed.
- Treat statements marked **To Test** as hypotheses rather than product guarantees.
